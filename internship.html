What I learned

Key ↳ Takeaways

The four principles I now carry into every AI-native content org I help build.
01 · CONTENT LEADERSHIP
Owning content means owning the pod
The output is the team. Shipping 350+ reels only worked because I set the priorities, unblocked handoffs, and owned the final review across 10–12 people.
02 · IP OWNERSHIP
Four voices, one editorial system
Ran @10xoperator, @decodingai.vs, @tessa.fairbrook and segments on the flagship as distinct IPs with a shared operating system underneath.
03 · AI-FIRST BY DEFAULT
99% AI-generated — visual, audio, motion
The whole studio ran on the AI stack. The edge wasn't the models — it was the prompt libraries and QC layer that made AI content stop looking like AI content.
04 · SYSTEMS > SPRINTS
Build the machine, then feed it
Turned research → brief → script → ship into templates. Post-mortems after every push. What used to be a hero effort became a boring, repeatable engine.
